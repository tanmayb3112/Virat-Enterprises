# Virat Enterprises — Marketing & Advertising Strategy

Goal: two revenue streams, marketed separately.
1. **Print jobs** (B2C + B2B) — fill the 6 Pune counters + online orders.
2. **Franchise leads** (B2B/investor) — ₹10–30 lakh tickets, Pune & beyond.

Everything below is free-tier or minimum-spend, matched to the website that's
already live (order wizard, franchise ROI calculator, WhatsApp funnels, Meta
Pixel + UTM capture already built in).

---

## 1. Positioning

- **Line for customers:** "Upload. Print. Delivered. — from ₹2/side, free
  delivery within 3 km over ₹500." Fast, cheap, reliable — the neighbourhood
  xerox shop that now takes orders online.
- **Line for investors:** "Own a Virat shop from ₹10 lakh — 6 shops already
  run on this playbook; the website routes online orders to your counter."
- The **black & yellow** brand is the visual shorthand for "xerox/print shop"
  across India — use it on every creative, board and pamphlet consistently.

## 2. Customer segments (print jobs) — who to go after, in priority order

| # | Segment | Why they buy | What they order | Where to find them |

**B2B motion:** email + WhatsApp + one physical visit with a rate card.
Offer a **monthly account** (credit + pickup) — that's the retention hook.

## 3. Channel plan (minimum budget)

| Channel | Cost | Use for | Cadence |
|---------|------|---------|---------|
| WhatsApp Business (free app) | ₹0 | Order intake, status, catalogs, broadcast lists (≤256/day per list) | Daily |
| Google Business Profile ×6 branches | ₹0 | "xerox near me" searches — the single highest-ROI free channel | Set up once, post weekly |
| Instagram/Facebook organic | ₹0 | Before/after prints, price posts, reels of binding/large-format | 3×/week |
| Meta ads — print jobs | ₹100–200/day | 2 km radius around each branch, students + offices | Always-on |
| Meta ads — franchise | ₹150–300/day | Maharashtra, 28–55, business/investment interests | 2-week bursts |
| B2B email (Gmail → later Resend) | ₹0 | The prospects database — sequenced outreach | 20–30/day max |
| /links page in Instagram bio | ₹0 | Already built | — |

**Do first, before any paid ads:** claim all 6 **Google Business Profiles**
(free) — photos, hours, services, the website link, and the WhatsApp number.
Most "xerox near me" volume converts here, not on social.

## 4. Offers that convert

- **Students:** "₹2/side B/W — show college ID, first binding free."
- **Coaching classes:** "Test-paper contract: fixed per-set rate + free
  delivery slot every morning."
- **Offices (law/CA):** "Monthly account — pickup twice daily, single invoice,
  7-day credit."
- **Franchise:** "ROI calculator + shop visit" (low-commitment CTA), then the
  ₹2L-fee/₹10L-total pitch in person.

## 5. Measurement (already wired into the site)

- Meta Pixel events: PageView, ViewContent (franchise), Lead (franchise form),
  Purchase (order value) — connect the Pixel ID env var and Ads Manager
  reads conversions automatically.
- Every lead/order stores its **UTM parameters** — tag every campaign link:
  `?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=print-students-01`
- Weekly review: cost per order ≤ ₹40 for print ads; cost per franchise lead
  ≤ ₹500; email reply rate ≥ 3%.

## 6. 30-day launch calendar

**Week 1** — Google Business Profiles ×6 · WhatsApp Business setup (catalog =
rate card) · Meta Business Manager + Pixel ID into the site · first 10 organic
posts scheduled.
**Week 2** — Print-jobs ad set live (₹100/day, 2 branches first) · email
sequence #1 to coaching classes + colleges (20/day) · physical rate-card visits
to 10 nearest coaching classes.
**Week 3** — Franchise campaign live (₹150/day) · email sequence #2 (law/CA
firms) · first-week ad data review, kill losing creatives.
**Week 4** — Scale winning ad set to all 6 branch radii · follow-up emails ·
first monthly-account contracts signed → case-study post.

## 7. Compliance notes (B2B email)

- Send only to **publicly listed business contact addresses**, one-to-one
  style, with a clear opt-out line ("Reply STOP and we won't write again").
- 20–30/day from Gmail keeps deliverability healthy; move to Resend with a
  proper domain + SPF/DKIM once volume grows.
- No bought lists — the prospect database is compiled from public sources
  with the source URL recorded per row.
